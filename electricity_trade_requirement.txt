# Electricity Demand and Supply Trade Management System

## Business Overview

The Electricity Trade Management System (ETMS) enables electricity operators to manage electricity demand and supply transactions between electricity suppliers and electricity buyers.

The operator acts as an intermediary and facilitates electricity trades in the wholesale electricity market.

The system should allow suppliers to submit available electricity capacity and buyers to submit electricity demand requests.

The system should automatically match demand and supply based on quantity, delivery date, and market region.

---

## Supplier Management

The system shall allow suppliers to register using:

* Supplier ID
* Supplier Name
* Contact Details
* Market Region

A supplier may submit one or more electricity supply offers.

Each offer shall contain:

* Offer ID
* Available Quantity (MWh)
* Delivery Date
* Price per MWh
* Market Region

The system shall store all submitted offers.

---

## Buyer Management

The system shall allow buyers to register using:

* Buyer ID
* Buyer Name
* Contact Details
* Market Region

A buyer may submit one or more electricity demand requests.

Each demand request shall contain:

* Request ID
* Required Quantity (MWh)
* Delivery Date
* Maximum Price per MWh
* Market Region

The system shall store all submitted demand requests.

---

## Trade Matching

The system shall automatically perform trade matching every 15 minutes.

A match shall be created when:

* Market Region is the same
* Delivery Date is the same
* Available Quantity is greater than or equal to requested quantity
* Supplier Price is less than or equal to Buyer's Maximum Price

When multiple suppliers satisfy the criteria, the supplier with the lowest price shall be selected.

A Trade ID shall be generated for every successful trade.

---

## Notifications

The system shall notify suppliers and buyers when a trade is successfully matched.

Notifications shall be visible within the application.

---

## Reporting

The system shall provide reports for:

* Open Supply Offers
* Open Demand Requests
* Matched Trades
* Cancelled Trades

Reports shall be downloadable in Excel format.

---

## User Roles

The system shall support:

1. Operator
2. Supplier
3. Buyer

The Operator shall be able to view all trades.

Suppliers shall only view their own supply offers.

Buyers shall only view their own demand requests.

---

## Audit Logging

The system shall maintain an audit trail for:

* Offer Creation
* Demand Request Creation
* Trade Match Creation
* Trade Cancellation

The audit log shall capture:

* User ID
* Timestamp
* Action Performed

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

* System shall support 1000 concurrent users.
* Trade matching shall complete within 30 seconds.
* System availability shall be 99.9%.
* User sessions shall expire after 15 minutes of inactivity.

# Online Banking Application – Fund Transfer Module

## Business Requirement Document

### Overview

The Online Banking Application allows customers to securely manage their bank accounts. This enhancement introduces a Fund Transfer module that enables customers to transfer money between their own accounts and to registered beneficiaries.

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-001: User Login

1. The customer must log in using a valid username and password.
2. After three consecutive failed login attempts, the account shall be locked for 30 minutes.
3. A successful login shall redirect the user to the Account Summary page.

---

### FR-002: View Account Balance

1. The customer shall be able to view all active accounts linked to their profile.
2. The available balance and account number shall be displayed.
3. Closed accounts shall not be displayed.

---

### FR-003: Add Beneficiary

1. The customer shall be able to add a beneficiary by providing:

   * Beneficiary Name
   * Account Number
   * Bank Name
   * IFSC Code
2. The account number must contain between 10 and 18 digits.
3. The IFSC Code must be exactly 11 characters.
4. A newly added beneficiary shall remain in "Pending Verification" status for 24 hours.
5. The customer shall not be able to transfer funds to a beneficiary in Pending Verification status.

---

### FR-004: Fund Transfer

1. The customer shall be able to transfer funds to a verified beneficiary.
2. The transfer amount shall be greater than ₹0.
3. The maximum transfer amount per transaction shall be ₹100,000.
4. The daily transfer limit shall be ₹500,000.
5. Transfers shall not be allowed when the available account balance is insufficient.
6. A unique transaction reference number shall be generated for every successful transfer.
7. The account balance shall be updated immediately after a successful transfer.

---

### FR-005: Transaction Confirmation

1. Before processing a transfer, the system shall display a confirmation screen.
2. The confirmation screen shall display:

   * From Account
   * Beneficiary Name
   * Transfer Amount
   * Transaction Charges
3. The customer must confirm the transaction before processing.

---

### FR-006: Notifications

1. A successful transfer shall trigger:

   * SMS notification
   * Email notification
2. The notification shall include:

   * Transaction Reference Number
   * Transfer Amount
   * Transaction Date and Time
3. Notifications shall be sent within 2 minutes of transaction completion.

---

### FR-007: Transaction History

1. The customer shall be able to view transaction history for the last 12 months.
2. The customer shall be able to filter transactions by:

   * Date Range
   * Transaction Type
   * Amount
3. The customer shall be able to download transaction history as a PDF file.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-001 Performance

1. Fund transfer processing shall complete within 5 seconds under normal load.
2. The system shall support 10,000 concurrent users.

### NFR-002 Security

1. All customer sessions shall expire after 15 minutes of inactivity.
2. Sensitive information shall be encrypted during transmission.
3. Users shall not be able to access another customer's account information.

### NFR-003 Availability

1. The application shall be available 99.9% of the time excluding planned maintenance windows.

---

## Assumptions

1. Customers already have active bank accounts.
2. SMS and Email gateway services are available.
3. Internet connectivity is available during transactions.
